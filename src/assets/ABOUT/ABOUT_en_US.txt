1. OVERVIEW AND TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

Eolo Platform is a native desktop application developed in Rust (Edition 2021) featuring an immediate-mode graphical user interface in egui/eframe (0.37) with hardware acceleration via wgpu. Designed to support academic study for students, researchers, and civil/mechanical engineering enthusiasts, the platform organizes didactic calculation routines into three analytical layers with 100% local execution:

1.1. Study Analytical Engine (Reference: ABNT NBR 6123:2023): Experimental routines for studying basic wind velocity (V0) using a reference dataset of 49 FAB weather stations across 27 Brazilian Federative Units, topographic factor (S1 — Ref. Clause 5.2), roughness, dimensions, and height factor (S2 — Ref. Clause 5.3, Categories I–V and Classes A–C), and statistical factor (S3 — Ref. Clause 5.4, Groups 1–5 and cladding reduction), estimating characteristic velocity (Vk = V0 · S1 · S2 · S3) and dynamic wind pressure (q = 0.613 · Vk²).

1.2. Educational Aerodynamics & Estimated Envelopes: Study routines for interpolating external pressure/shape coefficients (Cpe / Ce — Ref. Tables 6, 7, 8, 10, 14 and Figures 7–8) combined with internal pressure estimates (Cpi — Ref. Clause 6.3) for didactic simulation of sealed, uniformly permeable, or dominant-opening enclosures.

1.3. 2D/3D GPU Viewport, Study Reports & CLI: Real-time vector rendering of 2D cross-section frames and 3D orbital wireframe models with proportional vectors, accompanied by a study report generator exporting to HTML5 (A4), Markdown (.md), and JSON (.json), 5 educational benchmark presets, and the CLI utility (eolo-cli.exe).

================================================================================
2. THE 5 EDUCATIONAL TABS AND INTERFACE CAPABILITIES
================================================================================

2.1. Tab 1 — Location & V0: Selection of Brazilian State and reference FAB station, V0 setting, and educational configuration of factors S1, S2, and S3 displaying intermediate parameters (b, Fr, p, Vk, and q).

2.2. Tab 2 — Building Geometry: Educational parameterization of clear span (b), length (a), eaves height (h), roof pitch angle (θ), overhang width, and frame/purlin spacings.

2.3. Tab 3 — Openings & Cpi: Simulation of facade opening areas to study Cpi states and inspect estimated pressure (kN/m²) and linear load (kN/m) envelopes.

2.4. Tab 4 — Load Diagrams & 3D GPU: Interactive 2D/3D viewer with mouse camera controls (orbit, zoom, and pan) and instant switching between Dark and Light themes.

2.5. Tab 5 — ABNT Calculation Memorial (Study): Structured study report with section filtering, text search, and direct export to HTML5 (A4), Markdown, and JSON (always bearing non-production academic disclaimers).

2.6. Auxiliary Tools and Command-Line Interface (eolo-cli.exe): Includes dedicated modeless dialogs for Quick Calculation, Academic Traceability Matrix, Engineering Unit Converter, Interactive Manual, live bilingualism (`pt-BR` and `en-US`), bibliographic references of ABNT standards in the "Notices" tab, and the standalone `eolo-cli.exe` utility.
